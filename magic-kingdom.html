How to read the plan. Rope drop = be at the gate ~30 min before official open and walk straight to #57. The biggest time-savers to hit first or last: Seven Dwarfs (57), Peter Pan (45), Space Mountain (72). Midday is for A/C rest spots (green pills) while lines and heat peak.
Height reality check. A typical 7-yr-old (~48") clears everything here. A typical 5-yr-old (~43") clears the 32–40" rides but is under 44" (Space Mountain) and 48" (TRON) — use the Rider Switch rides so both adults still ride. Measure both before you go.
Lightning Lane is shown as typical assignments — ILL$ (Seven Dwarfs, TRON) is bought per-ride at park open for off-site guests; MP rides are covered by one Multi Pass purchase. Verify in the My Disney Experience app the morning of, along with live waits. Numbering follows Disney's Sept 2023 guide map.
