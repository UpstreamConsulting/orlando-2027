How to run Hollywood Studios. The two biggest waits are Slinky Dog Dash (42) and Rise of the Resistance (28) — rope-drop Toy Story Land for Slinky, and either rope-drop Rise or buy its Individual Lightning Lane. This is the day for your son's Savi's lightsaber build — reserve it ahead.
Height reality check. A typical 7-yr-old (~48") clears everything except maybe Rock 'n' Roller Coaster (48", borderline). A typical 5-yr-old (~43") clears the 32–40" rides but is under 48" for Rock 'n' Roller Coaster — use the Rider Switch rides. Rise of the Resistance (28) is intense and can intimidate young kids.
Evening: Fantasmic! (58) — arrive early for a seat. Waits are typical late-January estimates — check the My Disney Experience app the morning of. Numbering follows Disney's June 2025 guide map.
