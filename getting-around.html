ℹ️ What "non-Disney hotel" means for you

No Disney buses, monorail, or Skyliner to/from the resort, no 30-min Early Entry, and you pay park parking daily. You can still use Disney transport once you're inside the system (park-to-park, or the ferry/monorail from the Magic Kingdom parking hub).

Two things that touch every ride

🚗 Car seats (Florida law)

  • 5-yr-old: legally requires a restraint — including in Uber/Lyft.
  • 7-yr-old: legally exempt (age 6+), but a booster is recommended until ~4'9".
  • Best move: bring the 5-yr-old's own booster from BC — covers every leg, zero per-ride fees.
  • Backup: Uber Car Seat (+$10/ride). Lyft car-seat mode is not in Orlando.

♿ Scooter for the grandparents

  • Parks are 8–13 km of walking a day — strongly worth considering.
  • Off-site delivered (e.g. Buena Vista Rentals, serves Bonnet Creek): ~$30–40/day, keep it all week — resort, parking lots, park-to-park, even Universal.
  • Beats Disney's in-park $65/day rental (re-rent daily, stays in one park).
  • Be present for delivery (~4pm arrival); a minivan hauls it between lots.
