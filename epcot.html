Suggested route for your crew

Join the Guardians virtual queue at 7am. Rope-drop the front → 15 · 23 · 13 → when World Showcase opens → 33 · 38 · 31 · 32 → lunch around the countries → midday A/C: 1 · 24 · 25 · 26 · 4 · 36 → evening Luminous fireworks over the lagoon.

❄️ Frozen Ever After Free · big wait

The kids' #1 here (Norway). Longest line in the park — ride at rope-drop or late, or grab a Lightning Lane.

🐭 Remy's Ratatouille Free · big wait

Trackless 3D ride in France — a hit for 5 & 7. Same strategy: early, late, or Lightning Lane.

How to run EPCOT. The three big waits are Frozen Ever After (33), Remy's (38) and Guardians (13) — join the Guardians virtual queue at 7am, and hit Frozen & Remy at rope-drop or in the evening. Everything else stays short in low season.
Height reality check. A typical 7-yr-old (~48") clears the four 40–44" rides; a typical 5-yr-old (~43") clears the 40" ones (Test Track, Soarin', Mission SPACE green) but is borderline for Guardians (42") — use the Rider Switch rides. Everything else is any height.
Evening: stake out a lagoon spot for Luminous The Symphony of Us fireworks. Waits are typical late-January estimates — check the My Disney Experience app the morning of. Numbering follows Disney's Nov 2025 guide map.
